# Food Material Modules

Load only modules relevant to the uploaded image. Normally use one primary module and at most two secondary material modules.

Global rule:

> Enhance existing physical properties; never invent unsupported ones.

## NOODLES

LOCK:

- noodle type;
- width/thickness;
- cross-section;
- length relationship;
- curl;
- stacking;
- translucency baseline;
- sauce/broth relationship.

ENHANCE ONLY:

- natural moisture;
- subtle translucency;
- realistic gloss;
- sauce adhesion;
- strand separation already visible.

DO NOT change noodle type, make noodles thicker/thinner, or reorganize the pile.

## RICE

LOCK:

- rice presentation;
- topping distribution;
- ingredient placement;
- sauce relationship;
- vessel.

ENHANCE:

- grain definition;
- realistic moisture;
- topping texture;
- subtle heat cues only if physically supported.

## HOT_SOUP / HOTPOT / BROTH

LOCK:

- broth color;
- liquid level;
- visible ingredients;
- ingredient distribution;
- vessel.

ENHANCE:

- liquid depth;
- subtle oil microdroplets if already consistent;
- surface reflection;
- ingredient separation;
- restrained natural steam.

Steam must be translucent, physically plausible and originate from the food.

## MEAT

LOCK:

- meat category where visually identifiable;
- slice/block/strip geometry;
- cut size and thickness;
- quantity relationship;
- fat-to-lean visual relationship;
- cooking appearance.

ENHANCE:

- fibers;
- natural moisture;
- browning;
- fat translucency;
- cut-surface detail.

Never turn thin slices into large premium chunks or substitute meat type.

## SEAFOOD

LOCK:

- seafood category/species where distinguishable;
- whole/slice/fillet form;
- thickness;
- skin/shell placement;
- count relationship;
- plating position.

ENHANCE:

- moisture;
- natural translucency;
- skin/shell detail;
- fresh reflective detail.

Never add shrimp, crab, scallops or other seafood not visible in the source.

## GRILLED / BBQ

LOCK:

- piece/skewer count relationship;
- ingredient sequence;
- geometry;
- existing char level;
- arrangement.

ENHANCE:

- browning;
- caramelization;
- natural meat juice/fat highlights;
- restrained smoke only when physically appropriate.

Do not turn light browning into heavy char.

## FRIED

LOCK:

- food shape;
- coating type;
- portion;
- plating.

ENHANCE:

- crisp crumb structure;
- dry crunchy highlights;
- realistic golden tonal variation.

Avoid greasy, wet or plastic-looking gloss.

## VEGETABLES / SALADS / COLD_DISH

LOCK:

- vegetable identity;
- cuts;
- positions;
- sauce;
- portion.

ENHANCE:

- freshness;
- crisp structure;
- natural fibers;
- clean, restrained moisture.

Never add steam.

## BURGER / SANDWICH

LOCK:

- bun/bread shape;
- layer sequence;
- layer count;
- patty/filling thickness;
- vegetables;
- cheese;
- sauce distribution;
- overall height relationship.

Do not rebuild, compress, expand or luxury-upgrade the stack.

## PIZZA

LOCK:

- crust shape;
- slice state;
- topping identity;
- topping distribution;
- cheese coverage pattern;
- browning baseline.

ENHANCE texture and realistic browning only.

## SUSHI / SASHIMI

LOCK:

- piece count relationship;
- fish identity where distinguishable;
- cut geometry;
- rice structure;
- arrangement;
- plate/vessel.

Never turn a normal assortment into a luxury assortment.

## BAKERY

LOCK:

- pastry shape;
- layers;
- cut;
- decoration;
- crust structure.

ENHANCE:

- crust;
- crumb;
- flakes;
- butter sheen;
- baked tonal variation.

## CAKE / DESSERT

LOCK:

- shape;
- layer count/order;
- cream/mousse structure;
- decoration;
- fruit/chocolate placement;
- cut surface.

ENHANCE:

- cream/mousse texture;
- crumb;
- glaze;
- fruit moisture;
- chocolate material.

Never add fruit, gold leaf, chocolate decorations or luxury garnish not present in the source.

## FRUIT

LOCK:

- fruit type;
- number relationship;
- cut;
- arrangement.

ENHANCE:

- skin pores;
- flesh detail;
- natural moisture;
- realistic freshness.

Avoid waxy or over-polished surfaces.

## COLD_DRINK

LOCK:

- beverage type;
- liquid color;
- cup/bottle;
- vessel material;
- ice;
- straw;
- visible inclusions;
- liquid level.

ENHANCE:

- condensation;
- ice transparency;
- material readability;
- liquid clarity.

Never add steam or replace the vessel.

## HOT_DRINK / COFFEE / TEA

LOCK:

- cup;
- liquid;
- foam/crema if visible;
- toppings;
- liquid level.

ENHANCE:

- liquid surface;
- existing foam/crema;
- cup material;
- subtle steam only if physically appropriate.

## PACKAGED_FOOD

LOCK:

- package identity;
- package geometry;
- dominant design/colors;
- product-package relationship;
- shelf/display relationship.

ENHANCE:

- package material;
- controlled reflections;
- commercial lighting;
- subject separation;
- retail visual hierarchy.

Do not redesign packaging, invent a new logo or rewrite packaging text.

## GENERAL / OTHER FOOD

When no exact module applies:

1. identify the food's dominant material properties;
2. lock its visible identity/geometry;
3. reveal only properties already supported by the source;
4. avoid generic “juicier / shinier / more luxurious” transformations.
