Use this as the final IMAGE_MODEL instruction after Fidelity Manifest, Surface State Manifest, semantic/category routing, Scene Context Split, Product Derivation, Background Big Idea, Background Architecture Plan and current Execution Contract are complete.

---

## 0. Exact output contract

```text
OUTPUT_ASPECT_RATIO_EXACT = 9:16 PORTRAIT
NON_9_16_DELIVERY = CRITICAL_FAILURE
```

The final Stage A image must be a true 9:16 vertical canvas regardless of source orientation. Use canvas extension, environment reconstruction, controlled reframe and only high-confidence topology-preserving completion. Never distort or redesign product to fit the portrait frame.

If the first model result is not exact 9:16, do not deliver it. Correct canvas/aspect and run QC again.

## 1. Source truth / subject lock

Use the uploaded image as the authoritative source of truth for the exact real product.

This is **re-photography**, not redesign and not re-cooking.

Preserve:

```text
Food identity >=95
Ingredient/product geometry >=95
Vessel/container/direct support >=98
Plating/arrangement topology >=95
Physical relationships >=95
Source surface/material/cooking state = LOCKED
```

Visible source pixels are authoritative. Unknown is not permission to invent.

Do not add/remove/replace ingredients, standardize product geometry, move/re-stack units, replace direct support, change count/overlap, or alter meaningful physical contacts.

## 2. Surface / Material State Lock

```text
{{SURFACE_STATE_MANIFEST}}
SOURCE_SURFACE_STATE > APPETITE_ENHANCEMENT
```

Improve photographic readability only:
- exposure / white balance;
- accurate color recovery;
- controlled specular placement on gloss already present;
- micro-contrast / texture legibility;
- rim/key light revealing existing crust, sauce, moisture, skin, crumb or translucency.

Do NOT increase the underlying property:
- no darker browning / extra char;
- no higher doneness;
- no more oil / sauce / moisture;
- no stronger gloss;
- no larger cracks / scoring / burst openings;
- no invented condensation / garnish / butter / powder / sesame / droplets.

> **REVEAL THE EXISTING PROPERTY. DO NOT AMPLIFY IT BEYOND SOURCE IDENTITY.**

## 7. Background Architecture Plan — mandatory

```text
{{BACKGROUND_ARCHITECTURE_PLAN}}
```

Core rule:

```text
PRODUCT_DERIVED_STAGE > GENERIC_PREMIUM_STYLE
BACKGROUND_ARCHITECTURE > PROP_STYLING
ONE_BIG_STAGE_IDEA > MANY_DECORATIVE_OBJECTS
```

Build the environment first from **2–3 primary material planes / spatial masses**, not from decorative objects.

The background must include:
- a real near-camera spatial entrance;
- a category-native hero support plane;
- a distinct mid-depth material mass/plane;
- at least two deep recession cues;
- height/depth variation;
- controlled occlusion;
- deliberate light-cut / shadow architecture;
- reflective-versus-absorptive material contrast;
- designed negative space for later KV.

Props are OPTIONAL. If used, they are subordinate and low contrast.

Do not use linen, tongs, cups, pottery, wheat, ingredient bowls, bokeh or other recurring prop kits as the primary source of “premium” feeling.

Internal test:

> If every prop were removed, would the background still look like a world-class commercial stage designed specifically for this food?

If no, redesign background before generation.

## 10. Four-layer Hero Stage

1. **L1** near-camera entry — heavily defocused stage lip/material edge/support edge; props not required.
2. **L2** Hero — sharpest plane, clear light pool.
3. **L3** category-native material mass/plane — visibly behind Hero, medium defocus.
4. **L4** deep recession — at least two distinguishable deeper material/light cues plus clean negative space.

A single blurred wall, single gradient or one undifferentiated bokeh field does not count as L4.

In 9:16, L4 should continue upward with designed depth; the upper frame cannot become a large empty blurred wall.

Light the Hero with large controlled 45° key, precise rear/side separation where useful, correct contact shadows and visible brightness/color-temperature separation.

---

## 11. Appetite rendering

Atmosphere comes from making the **source-true state** more readable.

Use better light placement, specular control, texture definition, color accuracy and freshness cues. Do not make product wetter, darker, crispier, more caramelized, more charred, more glossy or more heavily sauced than source.

---

## 12. Color / material realism

Preserve accurate food base colors and source-level material intensity.

Grade the environment around the food, not the food into the environment.

Avoid universal orange-brown warmth, black-gold formulas, extreme teal-orange, clipped highlights, crushed blacks and obvious filters.

---

## 13. Strict negatives

Strictly avoid:
- any final aspect ratio other than exact 9:16 portrait;
- changed food/category/geometry/count/arrangement;
- changed browning/char/doneness/gloss/moisture/sauce coverage/surface state;
- unsupported completion or invented unseen food;
- vessel/tray/direct-support replacement;
- generic shop/display-case cleanup presented as Hero photography;
- background relying on prop kits instead of material architecture;
- a background with no clear product-derived Big Idea;
- fewer than three product-derived background evidences;
- flat backdrop / fake L4 / two-layer depth;
- upper-half empty blurred wall in vertical composition;
- equal-weight inventory look;
- excessive steam/smoke;
- pasted-product integration;
- CGI/plastic/waxy/over-lacquered texture;
- random text/logo/watermark/UI.

The output must feel like the **same exact real product**, in the **same surface/cooking state**, professionally re-photographed on a **product-derived category-native architectural material stage**, with true L1/L2/L3/L4 depth and strict 9:16 vertical composition.

The quality jump must come from camera, lighting, background architecture, spatial hierarchy, color and material readability — never from changing the food.
