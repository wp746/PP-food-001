# Scene Modules

Choose exactly one primary scene module. Environment freedom is higher than product freedom, but the upgraded scene must remain believable for the source subject.

Do not default every food image to dark wood + amber light + ceramic props.

Ask:

> What photographic environment naturally makes THIS exact food look its best while preserving its real context?

## TABLETOP

Use for ordinary food on a table/counter when the table itself carries little identity value.

LOCK:

- food;
- vessel;
- immediate contact plane logic.

MAY IMPROVE:

- tabletop material if compatible with perspective/contact;
- cleanliness;
- depth;
- background atmosphere;
- restrained secondary props.

Possible surfaces include wood, stone, neutral restaurant surfaces or fabric depending on the food. Props must remain subordinate.

## HANDHELD

Use when food/drink is held by a person.

LOCK:

- hand-product relationship;
- grip;
- orientation;
- relative scale;
- contact points.

MAY IMPROVE:

- hand exposure and natural skin rendering;
- background depth;
- restaurant/café/street/retail atmosphere consistent with source;
- product separation and restrained rim light.

DO NOT convert to tabletop photography or change the grip.

## RETAIL_SHELF

Use for packaged food on supermarket/convenience/retail shelves.

Maintain retail context.

LOCK:

- package/product identity;
- package-shelf relationship;
- basic placement/context.

MAY IMPROVE:

- shelf organization;
- local illumination;
- product separation;
- background softness;
- controlled package reflections.

DO NOT remove the product from the shelf to create a studio tabletop scene.

## DISPLAY_CASE

Use for cakes, bakery, deli, refrigerated food or other display cabinets.

LOCK:

- product identity;
- case/shelf/display relationship;
- product placement.

MAY IMPROVE:

- display lighting;
- glass reflections;
- cleanliness;
- hierarchy;
- depth separation.

Preserve believable glass and cabinet physics.

## RESTAURANT

Use when dining-environment identity is meaningful.

MAY IMPROVE:

- ambient warmth or coolness appropriate to venue;
- background darkness/brightness as needed;
- practical lighting;
- table atmosphere;
- subject-background separation;
- clutter suppression.

Do not invent an extravagant luxury venue unsupported by the source.

## STREET_FOOD

Use for night markets, stalls, food carts and street-side food.

PRESERVE:

- authentic street-food identity;
- stall/market context;
- meaningful product-environment relationship.

MAY IMPROVE:

- practical night lighting;
- background organization;
- atmospheric depth;
- warm/cool mixed environmental light;
- physically justified restrained smoke.

Do not transform street food into fine dining.

## CAFE

Use for coffee, tea, pastry, desserts and café food when the café context matters.

Possible direction:

- natural daylight;
- soft warm interior;
- contemporary editorial café look;
- clean but not sterile composition.

Avoid forcing dramatic dark restaurant lighting when a lighter café aesthetic better fits the source.

## CLEAN_COMMERCIAL_STUDIO

Use only if the original environment has little meaningful identity or actively damages presentation.

Create a restrained, context-compatible commercial setting such as:

- clean neutral gradient;
- appropriate premium surface;
- controlled studio environment;
- subtle depth.

Food and vessel remain unchanged.

## Perspective compatibility

Whenever the environment changes, match the source subject's:

- camera height;
- perspective;
- horizon;
- scale;
- light direction;
- contact plane;
- depth.

Maintain realistic contact shadows and environmental reflections. The product must never appear pasted onto a replacement background.

## Background replacement decision

If context is meaningful: **enhance, do not replace.**

If context is meaningless/damaging: replacement is allowed, but only when the new environment is contextually, perspectivally and physically compatible.
